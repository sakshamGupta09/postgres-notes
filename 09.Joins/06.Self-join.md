# Self join

- To compare rows within same table.
- There is no explicit join called self join. We can use INNER, LEFT, RIGHT or FULL join. It's just that the two tables we are joining are the same.

```sql
SELECT
	m1.movie_name,
	m1.movie_length,

	m2.movie_name,
	m2.movie_length
FROM movies m1
INNER JOIN movies m2 
ON m1.movie_length = m2.movie_length AND
m1.movie_id <> m2.movie_id
ORDER BY
	m1.movie_name ASC
```
